• Routes: each route with a VPN exit shows the address and country Cloudflare sees for it, and says when it isn't the country you chose. It's checked when the routes connect and when a server changes.
• Connections: a server that stops answering is noticed and replaced sooner. The route gets a minute to come back on the same server, then moves to another server of the same location.
• Imports: WireGuard configs keep their own PersistentKeepalive.
